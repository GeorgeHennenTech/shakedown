# SHAKEDOWN — Wardrobe Plan (first pass)

What the bible already says about clothes and looks, and a proposed first catalogue of about 120 pieces to build. Proposed items are suggestions for Seth to cut, add to or rename. *(2026-11-16)*

## 1. What the bible already has

Sources: Part 11 (Wardrobe & Fashion), Part 12 (Living Hair & the Wook Barber), the Maker path, Pins, Streetwise.

### Character creation
- **Appearance only.** You pick looks, a few basic starter fits and your DJ name. You do not pick a path or a DJ archetype.
- **Looks:** body, face, hair, skin, drawn from the same parts library that generates the crowd.
- **Optional origin pick:** bedroom producer, lot kid, burner, or trustafarian slumming it. Flavours early dialogue and gives a tiny starting nudge.
- **Hair is deliberately limited at the start:** texture, colour, a starting length (short to medium) and a few simple styles. Big styles are grown into and earned at the barber.
- **Not in the bible:** an age slider, a skin tone palette, a list of body types. These are new.

### Living hair
- **Length grows over time:** Buzzed → Short → Medium → Long → Very Long → Feral. Beards grow on the same clock.
- **Dreads happen on their own** if you stay funky and don't groom: Kempt → Unkempt → Tangled → Baby Dreads → Mature Dreads → the Legendary Wook Mane. Mature dreads are cut-only.
- **Barber Bodhi ("Split Endz")** cuts, shaves, starts and maintains dreads, combs out matting, braids, wraps, beads, mohawks, top-knots and dyes (including UV colours). "The Big Chop" is a ritual event.

### How the wardrobe works
- **Slots:** top, bottom, footwear, headwear, accessories, hair, body art.
- **Closet at camp**, sized by campsite tier (the ratty tent holds three shirts). A small bag loadout for quick swaps. Any clothing vendor is a fitting room.
- **Saved outfit slots:** Day Fit, Night Fit, Stage Fit, Rain Fit.
- **Clothes get dusty and muddy** over the weekend; wash at the river.
- **Your outfit is your wanted poster:** swap fits in a tent to lose security.
- **The crowd uses the same pieces**, mixed by archetype (wook, raver, flow artist, vendor, bro, fairy) and recoloured.

### What clothes do (light effects, not armour stats)
| Item type | Effect |
|---|---|
| Footwear | Stamina. Flip-flops are comfy until they snap mid-lot. |
| Hats, parasols, sunglasses | Slow hygiene and hydration decay in the sun. |
| Layers and jackets | Warmth for night and sunrise sets. |
| Rain poncho | Protection in the Storm event. |

| Faction-coded fit | Rep with that crowd: wook for the woods, clean for VIP and bookers. |

### Making your own
- **DIY:** tie-dye, sew on patches, add pins, distress, wire in LEDs. Ties to the Patch Jacket collectathon.
- **The Maker path:** a design tool for tie-dye (fold patterns, dye palettes) and patchwork (panels, patches, stitching); sell off a blanket, then a booth, then a brand.
- **Body layer:** hair colour, body paint, glitter, face gems, temporary tattoos, piercings, nails.

- **Elf Ears:** codes you to the forest and psytrance crowd; first of a fae line (horns, wings, tails, face gems).

## 4. The Free Pile as the crafting source (proposal, expands Seth's 2026-11-16 note)

Seth's direction: the Free Pile is where you pick clothes to craft with. Players on the vendor (Maker) route get more skilled and earn more from it. Players on any other path, such as DJ, can still make a one-off to wear or gift. Everything below is a proposal built on that.

### What the Free Pile is
- A tarp at the edge of Shakedown Street where people dump what they don't want. Everything on it is free.
- **It restocks:** a little each morning, more after a storm (people abandon wet gear), and a jackpot on Monday pack-out.
- **It's random,** and what you can carry away is limited by bag space.
- **Other people use it too.** NPC makers and thrifters pick it over, so early visits find better stuff.
- **Etiquette:** leave something when you take something and you earn a little Vibes. Clear the whole pile and the lot vendors notice.

### What you find
| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Blanks** (a whole garment to work on) | White tee, tank top, plain hoodie, flannel, work shirt, jeans, denim jacket, tube socks, bucket hat, canvas tote, bedsheet, long johns, old suit jacket, dad's polo, bank promo shirt, kids' superhero shirt |
| **Fabric** (cut up for panels and patches) | Tapestry offcut, corduroy trouser leg, velvet curtain, lace tablecloth, torn flag, towel, wool blanket, the graphic from a ruined band tee, a moth-eaten sweater |
| **Camp salvage** (big pieces, odd materials) | Collapsed tent, shredded canopy top, burst air mattress, sleeping bag, broken camp chair, tarp, hammock, pool float |
| **Trim** (small bits) | Buttons, zips, shoelaces, paracord, safety pins, loose beads from broken bracelets, stray patches, dead LED string, spent glow sticks, bottle caps, can pull-tabs, feathers, lost wristbands |
| **Oddities** | Single left flip-flop, one glove, one-lens sunglasses, half a costume, a wig, a stuffed animal, a lost totem pole |

Every find has a **condition** (Trashed, Worn, Decent, Mint) and may be **funky**, in which case it needs a wash at the river before you can work on it.

### What you can do to it (techniques)
| Technique | What it needs | Unlocks at |
|---|---|---|
| Cut and crop | Scissors | Anyone |
| Distress and fray | Nothing | Anyone |
| Safety-pin and tie | Trim | Anyone |
| Basic tie-dye (one fold, one colour) | Dye, bought | Anyone |
| Sew on a patch | Needle and thread | Maker 1 |
| Bleach and reverse-dye | Bleach, bought | Maker 1 |
| Multi-colour tie-dye, new folds | Dye | Maker 2 |
| Patchwork (join panels into a garment) | Fabric, thread | Maker 2 |
| Stencil and screen print | Ink, a screen | Maker 3 |
| Embroidery and beading | Thread, beads | Maker 3 |
| Tailoring (fix fit, change the cut) | Sewing machine at camp | Maker 3 |
| LED wiring | LED string, battery, Power module at camp | Maker 4 |
| Salvage builds (see below) | Camp salvage | Maker 2 to 4 |
| Sew in your own label | Label run, bought | Maker 5 |

### Salvage builds (the fun ones)
- Collapsed tent → rain poncho or windbreaker
- Sleeping bag → puffer vest
- Bedsheet → toga, kimono or wrap skirt
- Three flannels → patchwork hoodie
- Jeans → jorts, plus two denim patches left over
- Canopy top → waterproof bucket hat and bag
- Broken camp chair → sling bag with a cup holder
- Burst air mattress → shiny rave skirt
- Wool blanket → hooded cloak
- Lace tablecloth → festival top
- Bottle caps and pull-tabs → chainmail vest
- Stuffed animal → spirit hood
- Single flip-flop → nothing. It stays a single flip-flop. (Running gag, unless you find the right one.)

### The Maker skill ladder
| Level | Name | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scrapper | Patches and bleach. Sell off a blanket. One piece a day. |
| 2 | Stitcher | Patchwork, full tie-dye, first salvage builds. You start spotting Decent items in the pile. |
| 3 | Tailor | Printing, embroidery, tailoring. Pieces can roll "Well Made". A booth becomes worth it. |
| 4 | Designer | LED work and every salvage build. You spot Mint items. NPCs start asking for your stuff by name. |
| 5 | Label | Your own tag. Pieces can roll "Signature". Other vendors stock you. |

What rises with level: the number of techniques you can stack on one piece, the quality a piece can reach, how many you can make in a day, how good your eye is at the pile, and the price people pay.

### How it makes money
- **Price** = the piece's base value × quality × how many techniques are on it × your brand's reputation × demand (the bible's existing pricing model).
- **Free Pile materials cost nothing,** so early pieces are nearly pure profit. Dye, thread, ink, LEDs and labels cost money, so better pieces need reinvestment.
- **Faction-coded pieces sell to that crowd:** tie-dye to the lot, LED pieces to ravers, salvage and leather to burners.

### Everyone else (DJ and other paths)
- Anyone can use a craft table without taking the Maker route.
- **Limits:** the four "Anyone" techniques only, one technique per piece, quality capped at "Homemade", one or two pieces a day.
- **You can wear it or gift it.** It sells for very little off a blanket and can't be stocked in a booth.
- **Gifting is the point:** the bible says gifted clothes carry sentimental Cred, so a handmade gift should be worth more to a relationship than a bought one.
- **A handmade stage fit** still counts towards your recognisable look.
- **No lock-in:** if a DJ keeps crafting, they earn Maker levels like anyone else (the bible's "emergent identity" rule).
