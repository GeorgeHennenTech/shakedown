# SHAKEDOWN — Camp Gear Progression (draft 4)

What you can own at your campsite and the order you get it in. The bible gives the skeleton: five shelter tiers and seven modules. Everything inside each ladder is a draft for Seth to cut and change.

**Changes in draft 4 (Seth, 17 Nov 2026):** section 9 follows single items as they level up (your tent, stove, cooler and eleven more). Section 10 covers quest rewards, including trophies you set up at camp.

**Changes in draft 3 (Seth, 17 Nov 2026):** the Garden grows into a real food source (herbs, mushrooms, vegetables for a food booth or truck). Fun and games is dropped for now. A new section 7 shows the whole camp evolving stage by stage.

**Changes in draft 2 (Seth, 17 Nov 2026):** every ladder now has eight steps, not five. Camp lights are sacred and impossible geometry. The pink flamingo flock, the inflatable T-rex and the traffic cone are cut. Five new ladders are proposed beyond the bible's seven.

Prices are bands, not numbers: **$** pocket money, **$$** a good day's hustle, **$$$** a real investment, **$$$$** a milestone. ♻ means it can be built from salvage with the Craft skill.

## The rules the bible sets
- **Each module pays in a different currency** (cash, capacity, time, access, upkeep, social), so players upgrade by playstyle.
- **Camp buys time, capacity, safety and social pull.** It is not a cash multiplier; the sound system is.
- **Nothing is wasted.** Sell back for 40–60%, give away at the Gift Lot for Cred, or pass to a mentee.

## How you get gear
Buy it on Shakedown Street, build it from salvage, earn it from quests and friends, or find it abandoned.

### Garden
Seth's call: this one is a keeper. You grow herbs, mushrooms and vegetables at camp, and what you grow feeds your other paths. It ties straight into the bible's Food Vendor path (one griddle → a popular booth → a fleet of trucks) and the Wellness branch's foraging.

**What growing gives you**
- **Free ingredients.** Home-grown food costs nothing to cook with, so every sandwich or burrito is more profit.
- **A price bump.** "Grown on the lot" food sells for more and sells more often.
- **Healer supplies.** Herbs become teas, tinctures and smudge bundles for the roaming healer.
- **Gifts.** A tomato you grew is a better gift than one you bought.
- **It takes time.** Plants grow over real festival days, need water (your Hydration supply or the river), and can be raided, trampled or flooded.

| Step | Gear | What you can grow | What it feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A single potted cactus** | Nothing. It is trying | Morale |
| 2 | Herb pots | Mint, basil, cilantro | Better-tasting food; mint tea for Hydration |
| 3 | Grow bags | Healing herbs: sage, lavender, chamomile | The roaming healer's kit; smudge bundles |
| 4 | Buckets and a trellis | Tomatoes, peppers, beans | Burrito and grilled-cheese-with-tomato recipes |
| 5 | Mushroom logs and a shade tent | Oyster and shiitake mushrooms | Veggie burritos; a high-value food item |
| 6 | ♻ Vertical pallet garden | Lettuce, greens, strawberries | Salads and smoothies for the blender bike |
| 7 | Tiny greenhouse | Everything, through bad weather, and faster | Enough to stock a booth |
| 8 | Beehive and a raised-bed plot | Honey, plus squash and corn | A food truck's whole menu; honey to sell and gift |
| Wacky | **A scarecrow dressed as you** | Nothing | Stops garden raids; unnerves visitors |

**Food truck link.** At Garden step 7–8 you grow enough to supply a booth, and later a truck, from your own camp. The truck itself belongs to the Kitchen and Getting-around ladders; the garden is what makes its menu cheap and special.

**Theft and security (built 17 Nov 2026).** Nothing is stolen on Thursday; it starts Friday. All odds are first guesses.
