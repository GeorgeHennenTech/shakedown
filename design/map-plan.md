# SHAKEDOWN — Map Plan (first festival, the Forest)

The picture is `art/map/shakedown-aerial-map-v2.png`. North is up. This is the map for now; it will grow.
Every place the bible names is listed here with where it sits and what happens there. "Built" means it exists in the playable prototype in `game/`.

## Layout in one paragraph
You park in the south and walk north through the Main Gate. Shakedown Street runs straight up the middle. The Campgrounds fill the east side, the Main Stage Field fills the west. The Healing Village and Flow Arts Field sit above them, the River crosses the whole map, and the Renegade Woods and the Hill lie beyond it.

## Smaller places, by zone

**At the gate**
- **Main Gate** — the only legal way in. Built.
- **Patches** — stands just inside the gate; the game's help desk for the whole game. Built.
- **Buzzkill HQ** — security tent beside the gate. Officer Dan's post; where ejections and bans are handled.
- **Hole in the Fence** — far south-west. The fence-jump way back in after an ejection.
- **Creek Tunnel** — south-east. The lot-rat secret route under the fence.
- **Supply Gate** — south-east. The vendor supply truck you can smuggle in on (the Supply Run quest).

**Shakedown Street**
- **Stalls** — the eight wardrobe vendors plus food: grilled cheese, cold water, veggie burritos, tie-dye and tapestry, rave and kandi, heady glass, leather and burner, the costume shop, The Designer.
- **Pin Booth** — pins to collect, craft, trade and sell.
- **Record Crates** — crate digging: buy tracks and sample packs.
- **Free Pile** and **Craft Table** — free salvage and the bench where anyone crafts; the start of the Craft skill. Free Pile is built.
- **Gift Lot** — give gear away for Cred and an ally instead of selling it back.
- **Bodhi's Barber Chair** — one chair under a tie-dye tarp. Haircuts, dreads, beard work, and lot gossip.
- **Water Station** and **ATM** — hydration and cash.
- **Vinny's Corner** — the north end of the street. The Nitrous Mafia's spot: "3 for $20, NO DEALS!" Built (Vinny and his tank).
- **Whip's Van** — tucked behind the stalls. The rogue insider who back-doors you a tank.

**The Campgrounds**
- **Your Camp** — your ratty tent near the gate end. Grows through tent, canopy, van, RV, glamp dome.
- **RV Row** and **Glamping** — the later camp tiers, visible from day one as something to want.
- **Conspiracy Circle** — the campfire with the djembes; the showcase comedy quest.
- **Drum Circle** — a rung on the DJ ladder (Drum Circle Selector), and a place to hide from security.
- **Showers** — hygiene, and what slows hair turning into dreads.
- **13th Porta-Potty** — alone at the edge of the woods, north-east. The conspiracy guy's lore spot at 4am.

**Main Stage Field**
- **Main Stage** — built. The pit in front and a sound booth belong here too.
- **Side Stage** — your first official slot (Side Stage Sundae).
- **Artist & VIP** — fenced trailers behind the stage.

**Upper map**
- **Healing Village** — teepees and the Workshop Dome. Home of the roaming healer idea.
- **Chill-Out Tent** — also the medical tent. Where you wake after fainting, and the goal in a bad trip.
- **Flow Arts Field** — hoops, poi and fire spinning (now a Charisma performance line).
- **Swimming Hole** and the **footbridge** — the bridge is the way into the woods.

**Across the river**
- **Renegade stages** — three hidden clearings with sound systems.
- **The Wizard's Bus** — the school-bus glitch-hop wizard who teaches you to build your first rig.
- **Sunrise Stage** — the secret clearing for the sunrise set and The Master's Set pilgrimage.
- **The Hill** — the hilltop circle at sunset (the Breakthrough quest) and a sunrise viewpoint.

**Moving around**
- **Art cars and golf carts** — on the paths; you can boost one on the Streetwise path.
- **Totems** — everywhere; used to find friends in a crowd.

## Not on the map yet
These are in the bible but have no fixed place. Decide when the map grows.
- The campground sound-system spot (DJ rank 5) and your own renegade stage plot (the Stage Builder).
- The Lot Rats' safehouse (the thieves' crew).
- Secret sunrise viewpoints other than the Hill.
- The Road and the other festivals on the Tour (Desert, Beach, the Big Destination Fest). These are separate maps.

## Differences between the map and the prototype
- The prototype's Main Stage is north-west of the street and its River is due north; the map keeps the same compass directions but spreads everything wider.
- The prototype has no Silent Disco, Flow Arts Field, woods or Hill yet.
- The prototype's Healing Village is squeezed between the main stage and the river; the map gives it more room.
- The map draws Patches twice outside the gate. He belongs just inside it.
