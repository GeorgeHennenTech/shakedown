# The corner crew

A brainstorm with Seth, 19 Nov 2026. Nothing here is built. It follows the balloon corner and Whip-for-hire in `decisions.md`.

## What Seth has decided

- **Four stations** on your corner: one **fills** balloons off the tank, one **takes orders**, one **bundles and collects the cash**, one **hands them out**.
- **Every member is earned**, never simply hired off the lot. The thing that earns them can be a quest or system that does not exist yet.
- **Every member is a named character.**
- **Not Moonbeam.** He is the kitchen neighbour, not a corner man.
- **Wages by the night, never a share** (standing rule).
- **The Village Elder is a man.**

## How the stations work together

The line moves at the pace of its slowest station, so an empty or badly filled station is the bottleneck. You can stand in at any station yourself, and your own skills apply there: your fill minigame at the tank, your eye for undercovers at the front. The fourth hire is the moment you can walk away and leave the corner running.

Each station carries its own risk:

| Station | What a good one does | What goes wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Filler | Sets how fast balloons are made | Pops them and wastes gas; over-fills |
| Order-taker | Keeps the queue short, spots undercovers | Sells to one of Officer Dan's |
| Cash | Counts fast, keeps money off the corner | Skims; gets robbed |
| Runner | Hands out quickly, keeps people moving | Seen by security; hands one to a wobbly buyer |

## Who they could be, and how you would earn them

More candidates than seats, on purpose: who you end up with should depend on how you played. Characters marked (bible) are already named in the bible's roster.

### Filler

- **Whip.** Already earned: keep him out of trouble and win the showdown. He knows tanks. The natural first hire.
- **"Regulator" Reg**, a welder from town who refills your tank on the legit route. Earned by ordering from town three times and never haggling. Never pops one; refuses to work if the Elder has shunned you.
- **Lost Sock**, who runs the Open Decks tent. Earned by a set that scores 90% or better. Fills in time with whatever is playing, so he is fast when the Main Stage is loud and useless when it is quiet.

### Order-taker

- **Doc** (bible: the quiet Trip-Sitter). Earned by making it right with the Elder. He reads people: spots undercovers and cuts off anyone already wobbling, so fewer buyers end up on a cot. Slowest of the lot, safest by far.
- **Brody**, an undercover who was no good at it. Earned by spotting him and waving him off five times; he quits the force and asks for a job. Knows every tell his old colleagues have. Officer Dan takes it personally.
- **The Conspiracy Circle Guy** (bible). Earned by finishing the Conspiracy Circle quest. Sees undercovers everywhere: never sells to a real one, and turns away one honest buyer in four.
- **Willow** (bible: the nervous First-Timer). Earned through the tender-care thread of the Breakthrough quest. Nobody suspects her, so security heat stays low. Putting her on a balloon corner is the choice the Elder will throw back at you.

### Cash

- **Tiny**, the goon Vinny sends to collect at your camp. Earned after a truce or a win, by clearing the debt Vinny holds over him, or by healing the knee he keeps complaining about. Nobody robs him. He skims a little unless you pay him on time every night.
- **The Fence** (bible, Streetwise). Earned by selling him stolen goods three times, or by not grassing on him to security. Cash never sits on the corner: it is gone and clean by morning, for a cut of nothing, because the wage is his cover.
- **Marge**, the stage crew boss from the Monday-to-Wednesday shifts. Earned by working five crew shifts without being late. Counts perfectly and will not work a night you sold to someone who collapsed.
- **Your best customer**, the one who says "Two. No, one. No, two." Earned by selling him twenty balloons. Terrible with numbers; the till comes up short or over at random.

### Runner

- **Buck** (bible: the Naked Guy). Earned by keeping him covered in the opening and one favour after. Security will not go near him to search him. Draws a crowd, which is good for trade and bad for staying quiet.
- **Juniper**, someone you sat with as a healer. Earned by helping the same person twice. Hands out gently and sends the shaky ones to the Chill-Out Tent. Quits on the spot if the Elder's people start warning buyers off you.
- **The "Where's My Car" guy** (bible). Earned by finding his car. Fast, cheerful, and wanders off for an hour whenever he remembers the car.
- **Cedar** (bible: the Self-Proclaimed Shaman). Earned by out-talking him in the Breakthrough quest. Blesses every balloon, which is slow, but his buyers tip.
- **Sparkle**, a kandi kid. Earned by trading the five bracelets in the Good Neighbour quest and one more to her by name. Knows everybody; brings friends; far too young-looking for Officer Dan to ignore.

## Other ways a member could be earned

Not tied to one person; any of these could unlock a seat:

- **A debt cleared.** Pay what somebody owes Vinny, the Fence or the lot office.
- **A rescue.** Find them lost at 3 AM, or sit with them through a bad night.
- **A bet won.** A fill-off against Whip's cousin; a dance-off; a blind taste test at your kitchen.
- **A stranding.** On exodus Monday somebody's ride leaves without them. Feed them through the quiet days and they stay.
- **A set.** Play the tent well enough and somebody from the floor follows you home.
- **A sale milestone.** Your hundredth balloon; your first night with no undercover sold to.
- **A betrayal.** Someone Vinny threw away after the showdown.
- **A trade of favours.** The General Store clerk, tired of the till, in exchange for a week of buying bread from nobody else.
- **The Tour, later.** A member who only joins once you have a van and somewhere to take them.

## The Elder, and the fourth hire

Seth asked how this would go down if the Elder's confrontation came when the crew is complete.

The night your fourth member starts, the Elder leaves the Healing Village for the first time anyone can remember and walks the length of Shakedown Street to your corner. He joins the queue like anyone else. When he reaches the front he does not buy. He sets down a festival wristband, cut from somebody on one of his cots, on your crate.

Then he speaks to your crew by name, because he knows them. To the one you earned through kindness: "I watched you sit with that boy until sunrise. Now you hand these out?" That member wavers on the spot.

You answer for all four of them, with the same three roads the game already has, each now costing or saving people:

- **Make it right.** The crew works the Elder's way: water with every balloon, the order-taker cuts off anyone wobbling, the runner walks the worst to the Chill-Out Tent. The line is slower and half as many end up on a cot. Everyone stays.
- **Give it up.** The crew carries the tank to the river with him. Each of them lands somewhere better, and each of those is a small payoff later: Whip gets clean, the runner becomes a Village healer, the cash man runs the Village's bowl. The reward is the big one already in the game.
- **"It is only balloons."** He nods and leaves. The member who wavered leaves with him. His people warn buyers off your corner, and Vinny, who has been watching from across the Street, sees a crew of three.

Open question: whether this replaces the current trigger (12 balloons sold) or is a second, larger scene on top of it.

## A clash to settle

The bible already has a **Moonbeam**: "the giggling Spunion" of the Breakthrough Circle, alongside Cedar, Willow and Doc. The prototype's Moonbeam is your camp neighbour and kitchen hand, and now has a model. They are two different characters with one name. Either the neighbour is that same Moonbeam on a calmer day, or one of them wants renaming.
