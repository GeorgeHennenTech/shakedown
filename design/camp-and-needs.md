# SHAKEDOWN — Campsites and Needs (pulled from the bible)

Everything the bible says about (1) your own campsite, (2) other people's campsites, and (3) the bars you maintain and how. Items marked **(ours)** were added in this project after the bible, and are in `decisions.md` or `future-ideas.md`.

---

## 1. Your own campsite

**The basics** (the bible's one-line summary): sleep, store gear, cook, craft, recover needs.

### What you can do there
- **Sleep and nap.** Refills Energy and raises your Vibes baseline. Better shelter means better sleep.
- **Recover needs faster.** Shelter tier and the Comfort module speed this up, which gives you more active hours in a day.
- **Store gear.** The Camp Stash is separate from the bag you carry.
- **Keep your full wardrobe.** The closet lives at camp and grows with shelter tier ("the RV has a real wardrobe; the ratty tent holds three shirts"). Saved outfit slots.
- **Bank your cash in the Safe.** Banked money cannot be pickpocketed and resists raids.
- **Cook.** The Kitchen module feeds the food hustles.
- **Craft.** The crafting bench covers kandi and glass. **(ours)** Clothing, pins and salvage builds through the Craft skill.
- **Make music.** "Bedroom production": make your own tracks in your tent on a laptop. DJ rank 1 is literally "making tracks in your tent".
- **Run your own little sound system.** DJ rank 5 is Campground Soundsystem. The Power module runs your PA.
- **Charge devices.** The Power module also enables a phone-charger hustle.
- **Lie low.** Stay at camp to "go dark" and cool the Buzzkill meter (the Heat Death quest).
- **Stash loot and ditch hot items** before a pat-down (Streetwise).
- **Change your outfit** to break a description-based pursuit.
- **Display your pin collection.**
- **Decorate it.** Tapestries, lights and art raise the camp's "vibe rating".
- **Fast travel.** A high-tier or well-decorated camp becomes a fast-travel hub.
- **Share it.** In co-op, friends share a camp and form a "camp family" crew.

### Shelter tiers
| Tier | Shelter | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ratty borrowed tent | Slow needs recovery, tiny storage, no theft protection |
| 2 | Real tent + EZ-up canopy | Shade slows Hygiene and Hydration decay; basic storage |
| 3 | Car camp (van) | Lockable, more storage, better sleep, can move around the lot |
| 4 | RV | Fast needs recovery, big storage, onboard kitchen, AC that cancels heat waves, drives the Tour |
| 5 | Glamp dome / custom | Passive Cred and Vibes, draws NPCs, fast-travel hub |

### Modules (bolt onto any shelter)
| Module | Upgrades | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen | stove → griddle → full | Food-hustle output, quality and speed (cash) |
| Storage | cooler → lockbox → shelving | More vending stock; theft protection |
| Power | battery → solar → generator | Charges devices, runs your PA |
| Comfort | sleep kit, hammock, shade | Faster needs recovery |
| Crafting bench | kandi / glass | Unlocks and upgrades craft hustles (cash) |
| Hygiene | solar shower | Passive Hygiene |
| Aesthetic | tapestries, lights, art | Camp vibe rating: passive Cred, NPC draw, fast travel |

### The Safe
- Tiers: Lockbox → Cash Box → Safe → Floor Safe → Office Vault (Act 2).
- When it is full you are nudged to spend. True overflow goes to an offsite stash.
- Ways to skip the walk back: the van as a mobile safe, hiring a runner, auto-deposit for a fee, booker direct-deposit.
- Rule: you can only ever lose what you are carrying.

### Things that happen to your camp
- **Raids.** Thieves take loose cash and items, more so while you sleep at an unsecured camp. The safe, a lockable van and the anti-theft backpack upgrade protect you.
- **Storms.** Secure your camp (The Storm quest). A flooded tent soaks everything and tanks Vibes.
- **Ejection.** If security throws you out, your camp is behind the fence until you get back in.
- **A killer campsite you built** gives a small ambient Vibes lift while you are there.

### Selling or passing it on
Sell back for 40–60%, give it away at the Gift Lot for Cred and an ally, or pass it down to a mentee.

## 2. Other people's campsites

### Social
- **The Aux Circle.** Sit with a camp passing the aux cord. When it reaches you, play one of your tracks. Read the circle right and you earn respect and they share track IDs; misread it and you bomb.
- **The Conspiracy Circle.** The campfire with the djembes; the showcase comedy quest with a drum mini-game.
- **Drum circles.** Sit in, busk for tips (DJ rank 3, Drum Circle Selector), or hide in one from security.
- **Being welcomed into a circle** and **a deep 3am conversation** both raise Vibes.
- **Kandi and pin trading** with campers.
- **Gifting food or gear you made** raises Vibes.
- **Camp Crush.** Your next-tent neighbour; a slow-burn romance over the weekend.

### Helping
- **Mud Season.** It rained; rescue a camp's collapsing canopy.
- **Carry Them Home.** A friend overdid it; get them back to camp safe.
- **Trip-sitting** someone through a rough one.
- **The Scent of a Wook.** Find deodorant for a beloved, devastating wook.
- **(ours) The roaming healer.** House calls: treat sunburn, hangovers and sore feet at people's camps, and smudge a campsite to lift its mood.

### Music
- **Campground stages.** With a 4-channel controller you can play renegade and campground stages.
- **Aux Cord Wars.** Settle who controls the campground speaker.

### Outlaw (Streetwise)
- **Raid campsites.** Loot unattended tents and coolers for cash, gear, clothes, stock, even a generator. Serious Vibes hit if caught; "a dead-broke-only move".
- **Hide in a tent** to lose the Buzzkills.
- **Duck into a tent to swap outfits.**
- **Tap someone's power** or steal a rig component for your renegade stage.

### Mystery and scavenging
- **Ghost Camp.** A fully set-up camp, abandoned, no owner. What happened here?
- **(ours) Scavenging.** Abandoned campsites and Monday pack-out are sources of salvage for crafting.

### The neighbourhood itself
- A trashed campground with litter everywhere lowers Vibes while you are in it; a cleanup raises the zone's Crowd Vibe.
- Campgrounds are where pickpockets and raiders work on sleeping people.

## 3. The bars you maintain

The bible's main list is six meters plus money. Two more were added later in the bible itself.

| Bar | The question it answers | Shown? |
|---|---|---|
| Hydration | Are you physically OK and conscious? | Yes |
| Energy / Stamina | Can you keep doing things and perform? | Yes |
| Nourishment | Are you fuelled (and buffed)? | Yes |
| Hygiene | Do people want to be near you? | Yes |
| Vibes | Are people drawn to you? | Yes |
| Buzzkill | Is security onto you? | Yes |
| Bankroll | How much money you have | Shown as a number, not a bar (decision 52) |
| Body | Acute physical damage; mostly sits full | Only when it moves |
| Tolerance / Highness | Weed tolerance and current buzz | Mostly hidden; felt through warnings |

Not bars you top up, but tracked: **Cred** (lasting reputation), **Crowd Vibe** (a zone's mood), and the **suspicion meter** in the nitrous questline.

The prototype currently shows two: **Thirst** (Hydration, upside down) and **Vibes**.

### Hydration
- **Drops from:** sun and heat, heat waves, dancing, alcohol, "party favors".
- **If it hits zero:** you faint, lose time, may miss a set, wake at the Chill-Out Tent, and can be robbed while down.
- **Ways to maintain:**
  - Drink water: bought, from your own cold-water hustle stock, or from water stations.
  - The River or Swimming Hole (free).
  - The Chill-Out Tent.
  - Hydration-pack bag: slows the decay.
  - Shade: an EZ-up canopy at camp; hats, parasols and sunglasses.
  - RV air conditioning cancels heat waves.
  - Wellness skill branch: slower needs decay.
  - **(ours)** The cup-holder sling bag slows thirst.

### Energy / Stamina
- **Drops from:** being awake and active, sprinting, no sleep, comedowns, couch-lock from indica.
- **Low Energy:** worse DJ performance, slower running; total exhaustion can trigger a collapse.
- **Ways to maintain:**
  - A full night's sleep or a nap at camp; better in a van or RV.
  - Comfort module (sleep kit, hammock).
  - Caffeine: a top-up now, a crash later.
  - Boots over flip-flops.
  - Stretching before a long night.
  - Wellness branch: faster recovery and more active hours.
  - "Party favors" give stamina, with a hard comedown.

### Nourishment
- **Drops from:** time; cannabis munchies drain it faster.
- **Ways to maintain:**
  - Eat lot food: grilled cheese, veggie burritos, quesadillas.
  - Cook at your own Kitchen module.
  - Certain foods give temporary buffs; "a genuinely nourishing meal" also lifts Vibes.
  - Food gifted by others.
  - Rider food and the hospitality spread if you get into VIP.

### Hygiene
- **Drops from:** heat and dust, time, porta-potty incidents, mud.
- **Low Hygiene:** people keep their distance, vendors serve you slower, a stinky DJ clears his own front row, clean spaces (VIP, Healing Village) turn you away.
- **Ways to maintain:**
  - River rinse or dip (free).
  - Showers. **(ours)** The Showers on the map beside Porta-Potty Row.
  - Solar shower Hygiene module: passive upkeep.
  - Shade slows the decay.
  - Temporary masks: deodorant, fresh clothes from your wardrobe.
- **The twist:** funk is cred with the renegade-woods crowd, so staying dirty is a valid choice. Staying funky is also what grows dreadlocks.

### Vibes
- **How it moves:** it always drifts back to your personal baseline. Sleep and wellness habits raise the baseline; neglect lowers it. A run of bad moments can start a spiral you have to break on purpose.
- **Ways to raise it** (the bible lists about 60; these are the groups):
  - **Music:** a transcendent set, nailing your own set, sunrise sets, dancing, a perfect drop.
  - **Connection:** trip-sitting, handing out a free ticket, reuniting people, a real Festie Bestie, kandi trades, hugs, helping strangers.
  - **Care:** sleep, yoga and workshops, a river dip, massage or sound bath, water when you needed it, a nourishing meal, grounding, stretching.
  - **Nature:** a vista or hidden spot, sunrise and sunset, a starry night, a camp you built.
  - **Substances done right:** a good trip in a good setting, a shared ceremony, one balloon, coffee.
  - **Recognition:** ranking up, earning a title, being recognised, a compliment on your fit.
  - **Generosity:** fair prices in a crisis, the Gift Lot, paying it forward, finishing a DIY project, petting the festival dog.
- **What tanks it:** bad trips, dehydration collapse, exhaustion, hunger, sunburn, being robbed, betrayal, ejection, security hassle, train-wrecking your set, losing your phone or car, a flooded tent, isolation.

### Buzzkill
- **Rises from:** illegal hustling (balloons above all), theft, fighting, using in the open, getting caught by a narc.
- **If it maxes:** ejection, with escalating bans.
- **Ways to bring it down or dodge it:**
  - Lie low at camp to go dark.
  - Dump hot inventory before a sweep.
  - Bribe a guard.
  - Break line of sight and blend: a dense crowd, a drum circle, a tent, a porta-potty.
  - Change your outfit.
  - Skills: Streetwise slows heat; Charisma's Smooth Talker defuses the Buzzkills.
- **If ejected,** three ways back: buy back, sneak back, or earn back.

### Bankroll
- Earned through hustles, gigs and services; kept safe in the Safe. Being broke lowers Vibes.

### Body
- **Drops from:** chaining balloons, mainly.
- **Low Body:** you wobble, drop things, fall over, then pass out.
- **Recovers with:** rest, air, food, water and time. A B12 supplement clears the lingering "Tingle Fingers" debuff.

### Tolerance and Highness
- Tolerance rises each time you smoke and falls over clean days. A tolerance break resets it hard.
- Too high flips Vibes negative and leads to greening out. Recovery is the care loop: water, something sugary, fresh air, lying down, a friend or trip-sitter.

### One shared collapse system
Dehydration, exhaustion, greening out, nitrous blackout and the bad trip all use the same sequence: warning signs → tipping point → collapse (lose time, miss sets, wake elsewhere, can be robbed) → recovery through care, by you or by a friend you earned.
