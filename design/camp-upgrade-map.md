# SHAKEDOWN — Camp Upgrade Map

*Written 18 Nov 2026. A single map of everything at your camp that can get better over time: what is in the game now, what comes next, what it costs and what it does. It replaces the scattered ladders in `camp-gear.md` as the plan of record; that file keeps the history and the tuning notes.*

## Built on 18 Nov 2026 (second pass)

Built as rules and data, with plain shapes for the 3D, so none of it is thrown away by an engine change. Crops, mushrooms and fillings live in `game/data/garden.json`, which any engine can read as data tables. The fourteen journal illustrations are 2D art in `art/journal/` (full size) and carry over as they are.

- **Garden.** Up to six plots (second planter $40, grow bags $90, raised bed $180 at Leather & Burner). Herbs, tomatoes, peppers and sage; seeds at the General Store. Watering can and compost bin at the workbench.
- **Recipes from crops.** Nothing to buy. The best filling you have goes into each sandwich by itself: morel +$5, chanterelle +$4, oyster +$3, tomato +$2, pepper +$2, herbs +$1.
- **Kitchen levels 5 to 7.** Butter dish and spatula ($40), flat-top griddle ($220, two sandwiches a flip), sandwich press ($90, two fillings).
- **Mushrooms, medium size.** A shelf built at the workbench (two spots, then four). Six grown species with their effects, including golden caps and the Trip; eight wild species with two lookalike pairs; the field journal on J with spore prints; the Elder's shop of cultures; the Vision Quest after a good trip.
- **Narcs are subtler.** They now talk like any keen buyer. You notice one by a detail (new boots, an earpiece): 30% of the time, more with Charisma, always with a lookout or a clear head.
- **Clear Head, reworked.** Four hours of: half again as much skill xp, reading what a buyer wants before you offer, no pickpockets, slower timing in the cooking game, and knowing any wild mushroom on sight.

Not built from this map yet: the car, storage and comfort pieces, power, selling from camp.

## Selling from camp, and hiring Moonbeam (plan, 18 Nov 2026)

Seth asked whether a good enough kitchen and garden should let you sell from your campsite, with Moonbeam hired to take orders and replant. Yes, as the step between walking sandwiches around and owning a food truck. It needs the crowd to move first, so it is built after that.

- **Opening the camp kitchen.** Needs the griddle (stove level 6), four garden plots and a hand-painted sign. People passing the Campgrounds then walk up and order.
- **Orders.** Each customer wants a particular sandwich. Filling orders from what you have cooked is quick money; cooking to order pays more and takes your time.
- **Hiring Moonbeam.** At friendship 4. A share of the day's takings (a fifth, to be tuned), not a wage. One job at a time, which you choose each morning:
  - *Front of house:* takes orders and sells from your stash while you are out, at a lower rate than you would manage yourself.
  - *Garden:* waters, harvests and replants, so plots never sit empty.
- **A second hand later.** Somebody you have healed or helped can take the other job, so both run at once.
- **Limits that keep it honest.** Moonbeam only sells what you cooked, so you still have to cook. An open kitchen draws more attention: raids and security sweeps are likelier while the sign is up.
- **Where it leads.** A camp kitchen with two helpers is the business a food truck is bought to carry onto Shakedown Street.

### The Trip (golden caps)

Abstract on purpose: no doses, no method, one "handful" a day.

- **Set and setting decide it.** Where you are, who is with you and how your bars stand are added up when you eat them.
- **A good trip** (Healing Village or your own camp, Vibes up, fed and watered, Moonbeam or the Elder sitting with you): the colours of the world breathe for a while, people say things that are either profound or very funny, Vibes fill, you bank a skill point, and every wild mushroom on the map shows itself. A good trip is also what opens the **Vision Quest**, the once-a-festival respec from decision 51.
- **A bad trip** (Shakedown Street at midnight, Vinny's heat high, hungry, alone): the bible's Bad Trip Spiral. Vibes crash, the screen closes in, you lose hours, and you need someone to sit with you. A friend who comes is the same care-as-gameplay the showdown uses.
- **It is illegal on the lot.** Growing a tub is a quiet risk: a security sweep of an unguarded camp can find it. Selling them works like balloons, narcs included, and the Elder will not stand with someone who deals them.
- **The Elder teaches the safe way.** Her short quest, "Set and Setting", is how you learn the rules above before you can get it wrong.

### The field journal

- A page per species, fourteen in all, opened from the bag.
- **Finding one** starts its page: a drawing, and where and when you found it.
- **Checking it** (look underneath; cut it open; watch it at night) adds the tells. **A spore print**, left overnight at camp, finishes the page.
- A finished page means you never mistake that species again, and it unlocks its recipe, tea or use.
- Pages pay Wellness xp. All six grown, or all eight wild, pays a skill point. The full book is a title on the Monday report.
