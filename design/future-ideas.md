## Scavenge-and-craft gear that helps you elsewhere
*(Seth, 16 Nov 2026 — sparked by the camp-chair sling bag with the cup holder in the salvage-builds lookbook sheet.)*

**The idea.** Crafting is its own skill that levels up by doing it. You scavenge garbage (the Free Pile, overflowing bins, abandoned campsites, Monday pack-out), take it to a crafting table, and make items that are useful, not only cosmetic. What you make helps you progress in other parts of the game.

**How it fits what we have.**
- Free Pile finds, techniques and the Maker ladder are in `wardrobe-plan.md` §4. That section is about clothes you wear or sell. This idea adds **function**: crafted items carry small stat effects.
- The bible already has the backpack upgraded by "craft / modular" pouches, a crafting bench for kandi and glass, pin crafting, and the 7-branch skill tree with learn-by-doing. It has no crafting branch and no scavenging loop.
- Picking up trash already raises Crowd Vibe in the bible, so scavenging can pay twice: materials for you, a cleaner zone for everyone.

**Example crafted items and what they help (all numbers to be tuned in playtest).**

| Item | Made from | Helps |
|---|---|---|
| Cup-holder sling bag | broken camp chair | carry slots; a held drink slows thirst (Wellness) |
| Tent-fly rain poncho | collapsed tent | no "soaked" penalty in storms |
| Sleeping-bag puffer vest | sleeping bag | cold nights drain energy slower |
| Tarp shade rig | tarp + tent poles | campsite rest recovers faster; stall customers linger (Hustle) |
| Bottle-cap chainmail vest | bottle caps | Cred with the burner crowd (Charisma) |
| Cooler-lid serving tray | cracked cooler | serve food orders faster (Hustle) |
| Hidden-pocket patchwork hoodie | flannels | harder to pickpocket; smuggle one item (Streetwise) |
| Fairy-light totem | dead fairy lights + pole | friends find you in a crowd; small Vibe boost near a stage |
| Cardboard record crate | boxes + duct tape | carry more vinyl to a set (Decks) |

**Open questions.**
- *Settled in decision 51:* crafting is its own branch (Craft) with a numbered level, and scavenging is its early tiers.
- Do crafted items wear out, so there is a reason to keep making them?

## The roaming healer
*(Seth, 16 Nov 2026.)*

**The idea.** On the Healer / Wellness path you walk the festival healing people where they are, using eastern medicine and burning sage, not only from a tent in the Healing Village.

**What the bible already has.** Healing Village activities (yoga, meditation, sound baths, breathwork, ecstatic dance), paid "vibe services" (tarot, palm readings, sound baths, energy work, massage), teaching workshops, trip-sitting, and the Wellness branch ending in Caretaker and The Anchor. All of it is tied to the village or to rescuing someone in a bad trip.

**What is new.**
- **House calls.** People across the grounds have visible ailments (sunburn, hangover, sore feet from dancing, bad vibes at a campsite, post-set ringing ears). You spot them and treat them on the spot.
- **A healer's kit you carry.** Sage bundle, herbs and tinctures, acupressure, cupping, a singing bowl. Each treats different ailments and is a short mini-game.
- **Smudging a place, not just a person.** Burning sage at a campsite or stage corner lifts that spot's Crowd Vibe for a while.
- **Foraging feeds it.** Herbs gathered in the woods (already in the Wellness branch) become your supplies, so it links to crafting.
- **Payment in the gift economy.** Tips, trades and Cred more than cash, with regulars who come looking for you.

**Open questions.**
- Played straight, or with some satire (the healer whose sage sets off a tent, the guru who is clearly a fraud)?
- Does healing actually fix needs, or mainly raise Vibes?

## VJs play the dome
*(Seth, 17 Nov 2026.)*

**The idea.** VJs should be able to perform in the Workshop Dome in the Healing Village.

**Why it fits.** A geodesic dome is a natural projection surface: visuals can wrap the whole inside. The bible already has VJ / Visual Artist as a full path ("visuals-for-hire → a signature look → the studio every stage wants") and the new Tech branch covers running visuals, but neither names a venue where a beginner VJ plays. The dome could be that first venue, the way the Silent Disco is for DJs.

**Open questions.**
- Is the dome a VJ-only gig, or a VJ paired with a sound bath or an ambient DJ set?
- Daytime workshops and night-time visuals in the same dome, or a second dome?

## The devious build is the best at VIP
*(Seth, 17 Nov 2026.)*

**The idea.** A devious, Streetwise-leaning character should be better at sneaking into the Artist & VIP area and should get more out of it once inside: free food and the other amenities.

**What the bible already has.** Streetwise lists "sneak into VIP / backstage / the artist area — network with bookers, raid the hospitality spread, meet headliners, dig for rare track IDs", and credential forging is a Streetwise tier. VIP is otherwise locked behind reputation.

**What is new.** Making it a real advantage of the build, not just a one-off stunt:
- Higher Streetwise = easier entry (forged or lifted credentials, slipping past the gate) and longer before you are noticed.
- Inside, the devious player uses amenities the legit guest also gets, without having earned the rep: rider food (Nourishment), clean showers (Hygiene), shade and cold drinks (Hydration), a quiet couch (Energy).
- Getting caught should cost Buzzkill heat and Cred with bookers, so it stays a risk.

**Settled (Seth, 17 Nov 2026).** Legit access to VIP and its amenities only comes once a DJ is playing big gigs. Until then, sneaking in is the only way, which is what makes the devious build's early access valuable.
